Understanding GAFI Company Incorporation in Egypt

  1. El Gendy & Partners Legal Team
  2. Corporate Law Guide

Establishing a commercial entity in Egypt starts with selecting the right corporate vehicle under Egypt's Companies Law and Investment Law. Whether founders are launching a single-person company, a limited liability company (LLC), a partnership, or a joint-stock company (JSC), early structuring decisions shape governance, capital obligations, tax registration, and future investor readiness.

Most company formations in Egypt are processed through the General Authority for Investment and Free Zones (GAFI), which coordinates commercial name clearance, articles of incorporation authentication, commercial registry issuance, and tax card registration.

Well-drafted articles of incorporation and shareholder agreements prevent governance deadlocks, clarify management authority, and ensure regulatory filings proceed without avoidable rejections or delays.

Before initiating incorporation, founders should prepare clear details regarding the company's commercial objectives, registered office lease, shareholding percentages, authorized signatories, and identification or corporate extracts for all local or foreign partners.

Key steps in the Egyptian incorporation process

Working with Egyptian corporate counsel helps align every statutory step from initial structuring through post-incorporation licensing:

  • Choose the appropriate legal form (LLC, joint-stock company, single-person company, or partnership).
  • Obtain a non-confusion certificate for the proposed commercial trade name.
  • Draft and review the articles of incorporation, bylaws, and shareholder arrangements.
  • Complete GAFI review, authentication, Commercial Registry issuance, and tax card registration.
  • Secure sector-specific commercial licenses and establish ongoing corporate governance compliance.
