Economic courts & commercial litigation

Claims filed before Egypt's Economic Courts first pass through a preparatory chamber (Hay'at Al-Tahdir) to organize pleadings, review evidence, and explore amicable settlement. Unsettled disputes are referred to first-instance or appellate Economic Court circuits according to statutory jurisdiction.

Egyptian Economic Courts have specialized jurisdiction over disputes governed by key economic statutes, including company law, capital markets, banking operations, bankruptcy and restructuring, intellectual property, telecommunications, and technology transfer.

Yes. We represent businesses and financial stakeholders in commercial cheque enforcement and defense, credit facility disputes, letters of guarantee, and banking litigation before Egyptian courts.

Yes. We advise creditors and debtors on Egypt's Restructuring, Preventive Composition, and Bankruptcy Law, handling court filings, restructuring plans, and creditor claim protection.

Yes. We handle trademark, copyright, trade secret, and commercial IP infringement claims and defenses before the Economic Courts.
