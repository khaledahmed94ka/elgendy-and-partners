Establishing a manufacturing or commercial enterprise in Egypt requires careful alignment between business objectives, corporate structure selection, and regulatory filings. This illustrative overview demonstrates how El Gendy and Partners typically guides a Cairo-based manufacturer or investor through end-to-end GAFI company formation, corporate bylaws drafting, and commercial registry licensing. To maintain strict attorney-client confidentiality, this page presents a generic service scope rather than a record of a specific client engagement.

Our legal approach

From initial structuring through post-incorporation compliance, our corporate counsel coordinates each statutory milestone so founders and shareholders enter operations on a clear legal footing under Egyptian company and investment laws.

Selecting the appropriate legal form and drafting compliant articles and bylaws.

Managing GAFI filings, commercial registry issuance, and licensing requirements.

Key procedural deliverables

A structured incorporation mandate typically encompasses the following legal and regulatory steps:

  • Evaluating LLC, joint-stock, partnership, or single-person structures.
  • Drafting bilingual Arabic and English articles, bylaws, and shareholder agreements.
  • Completing GAFI submission, commercial registry extract, and tax card setup.
  • Advising on ongoing governance, commercial contracts, and FRA/EGX readiness where applicable.

Addressing corporate governance, shareholding mechanics, and licensing requirements at formation helps businesses minimize regulatory delays and avoid future shareholder or commercial disputes.
