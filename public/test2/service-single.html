El Gendy and Partners - Law Firm & Legal Consultants (الجندي للمحاماة والاستشارات القانونية) advises Egyptian companies, founders, investors, and individual clients across four specialized practice areas spanning corporate advisory, capital markets compliance, and courtroom litigation.

1. Corporate Law & Incorporation

We guide entrepreneurs, domestic businesses, and foreign investors through every legal stage of establishing and operating commercial entities in Egypt.

  • Formation of all company types (capital companies, partnerships, single-person companies, joint-stock companies)
  • Drafting and reviewing articles of incorporation, bylaws, and commercial contracts
  • Commercial licensing, commercial registry, and tax card issuance
  • Representation and filings before GAFI (General Authority for Investment)

2. Capital Markets & Securities

Our capital markets counsel supports issuers, shareholders, and corporate boards in structuring transactions and maintaining full regulatory compliance in Egypt.

  • Legal advisory for public offerings, acquisitions, and mergers (M&A)
  • Compliance with the Financial Regulatory Authority (FRA) and the Egyptian Exchange (EGX)
  • Corporate governance frameworks and board advisory
  • Investor relations documentation and shareholder disclosures

3. Economic Courts & Commercial Litigation

We represent businesses and financial stakeholders in high-stakes commercial disputes before Egypt's specialized Economic Courts.

  • Litigation before first-instance and appellate circuits of the Economic Courts
  • Bankruptcy and commercial restructuring proceedings
  • Commercial cheques and negotiable instrument disputes
  • Intellectual property rights, banking, and commercial contract disputes

4. Criminal & Civil Litigation

Our litigation lawyers deliver thorough defense and civil courtroom representation for corporate officers, institutions, and private clients.

  • Public funds cases, financial and commercial crimes, and corporate crimes
  • Civil lawsuits and compensation / damages claims
  • Real estate ownership, lease, and development disputes
  • Contractual and civil obligation disputes before Egyptian courts
